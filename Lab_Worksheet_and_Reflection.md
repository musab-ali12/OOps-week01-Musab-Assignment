## Short Reflection

This lab helped me see that objects in programming are similar to everyday things. A car, for example, has its own information such as colour, fuel and speed, while it can also perform actions such as starting, driving and turning. I learned that a class is like a blueprint, while each object created from that class can have different values. In my program, the BMW and Honda Civic are both Car objects, but they keep separate states. I also understood why `this` is useful when a constructor or method parameter has the same name as a field.
